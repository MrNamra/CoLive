# Share Your Workspace

Turn your open editor workspace into a live collaborative session with a single click.

1. Open a folder in your editor.
2. Click the **Share Workspace** button in the CoLive Activity Bar view, or run `CoLive: Share Workspace` from the command palette (`Ctrl+Alt+L S`).
3. An invite code and deep link are automatically generated and copied to your clipboard.
4. Share the link or paste code with teammates anywhere in the world.
5. When a guest requests to join, you will receive an approval notification with options to grant Read & Write access, Read-only access, or Decline.
